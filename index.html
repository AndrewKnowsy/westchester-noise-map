    Noise level (dBA, 24-hour average)

      Levels are modeled 24-hour equivalent A-weighted sound levels (LAeq, dBA), not measurements. Each grid cell is about 30 m across. The model covers road, rail and airport traffic only (no helicopters, construction or other sources). It does not account for noise barriers or terrain, so shielded areas may be overestimated. Anything under 45 dB is left blank.

      The 2022/23 release models 2022 aviation and rail traffic and 2023 road traffic. Switch to 2020 to compare.
